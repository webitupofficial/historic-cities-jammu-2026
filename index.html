The idea

One city, read through three themes

The Historic Cities Series is a national initiative led by the National Institute of Urban Affairs. The Jammu Edition explores the city's layered urban identity through three threads.

01

Riverine Memory

The Tawi, and the life that has gathered along its banks.

02

Plural Heritage

Temples, shrines, palaces and bazaars — many traditions sharing one city.

03

Community Narratives

The city as told by the people who live in it.

Community storytelling

Echoes from the River: Stories of Jammu

Join our session focused on capturing and documenting community narratives, oral histories and personal memories associated with Jammu's landmarks and the River Tawi. This session celebrates the intangible cultural heritage that lives in the voices of its people.

Selected stories and interviews will be digitally archived and featured in our forthcoming documentary series, ensuring your family's history becomes part of the city's documented legacy.
