Essence of the City

Seven ways to read Old Jammu

The city is more than a collection of historic buildings. It is a living cultural landscape shaped by memories, traditions, stories, sounds, foodways, languages and everyday experience. Student teams document it through seven themes.

01

City Tales & Street Stories

Local narratives, neighbourhood histories, legends and everyday stories of streets, bazaars and public spaces.

02

Oral Histories & Community Memory

Conversations with residents, traders, artisans, religious functionaries and elders about the city they remember.

03

Languages & Expressions

Dogri and the other languages of the city — local vocabulary, sayings, greetings and linguistic traditions.

04

Food Heritage

Traditional cuisines, local food practices, iconic eateries, festive foods and culinary memories.

05

Clothing & Cultural Identity

Traditional attire, adornments, occupational dress and changing patterns of self-representation.

06

Sounds & Sensory Landscapes

Temple bells, devotional music, vendors’ calls, smells and textures that give the city its character.

07

Visual Archives & Urban Memory

Archival and family photographs, architectural details, signage and other visual traces of the city’s past.

Workshops

From training to the field

Two workshops prepare students to record the living heritage of Old Jammu. What they collect becomes the immersive kiosks and displays of the exhibition.

  1. 01

    Online · 2 hours · Facilitated by the City Coordinators

    Student Training Workshop

    Methods of heritage documentation: oral history, interviewing community members, photo, video and audio recording, field observation, and the ethics of working with local communities.

    19 Nov 2026
  2. 02

    10:30 am – 4:00 pm · Panjbakhtar Mandir, Old City

    Documentation Workshop

    Thematic teams fan out across the Old City from Panjbakhtar Mandir, the central coordination point, to document its people, places, sounds and crafts.

    21 Nov 2026
  3. 03

    Exhibition

    Essence of the City

    The “soul of the city” told through multiple senses — Old Jammu presented through the voices, memories and cultural practices of its people.

    Dates soon
